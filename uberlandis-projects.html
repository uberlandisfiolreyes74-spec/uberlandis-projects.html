SMA-03, MSCA v6, and EGDS form a unified decision science stack — from risk measurement to option evaluation to temporal governance.

Framework 01
SMA-03 v3
Irreversibility Index · Lock-in Risk Measurement
Quantifies organizational lock-in through the II-Score (0–1 range), five risk tiers, and temporal decay parameter λ. Empirically validated on 120 organizational decisions. Integrates ODE modeling and ML ensemble classification for real-time risk assessment.
KEY FORMULA: E[V]_adjusted = E[V] / (1 + II²)
Framework 02
MSCA v6
Monte Carlo Scenario Analysis · Option Evaluation
Probabilistic option evaluation through Monte Carlo simulation, adjusted expected value computation, and Pareto frontier optimization. v7 roadmap includes causal inference (DoWhy), game-theoretic modeling, and Extreme Value Theory over an 18-month timeline.
12 IDENTIFIED LIMITATIONS → V7 ROADMAP DEFINED
Framework 03
EGDS
Execution-Gated Decision System · Temporal Governance
Structures decision implementation through temporal checkpoints at Days 7, 30, 60, and 90. Gates execution based on reversibility windows, ensuring decisions are re-evaluated as new information emerges before irreversibility thresholds are crossed.
CHECKPOINTS: D7 · D30 · D60 · D90
